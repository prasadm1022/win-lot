# WinLot

**Visit the site: [https://winlot.hadesvault.com](https://winlot.hadesvault.com)**

WinLot is a free website that keeps the full history of winning numbers for Sri Lanka's most
popular lotteries and turns it into easy-to-read charts and suggested numbers for the next draw.

## What lotteries does it cover?

- **National Lotteries Board (NLB):** Dhana Nidhanaya, Mega Power, Govisetha
- **Development Lotteries Board (DLB):** Ada Kotipathi, Super Ball, Shanida, Kapruka

## What can you do on WinLot?

- **See the latest results** for every lottery on one page, with the winning letter and numbers
  shown as lottery balls.
- **Get suggested numbers for the next draw.** For each lottery WinLot offers a few sets of numbers
  (and a letter) chosen from patterns in past results, with a countdown to the next draw.
- **Explore the history.** Browse every past draw, going back to 2021 for DLB lotteries.
- **Understand the patterns** through colourful charts: which numbers come up most often ("hot"),
  which come up least ("cold"), which have not appeared for a long time ("overdue"), common number
  pairs, how often each letter wins, and more.
- **Use it anywhere.** It works on phones, tablets and computers, in a dark or light look.

## How does it work?

1. **It collects the results.** Every evening, after the draws, WinLot reads the official results
   published on the NLB and DLB websites and adds them to its own archive. Nobody has to type them in.
2. **It studies the history.** Each number gets a score based on how often it has appeared over the
   years, how often it has appeared recently, and how long it has been since it last appeared.
3. **It suggests numbers.** Using those scores, WinLot builds a few balanced sets of numbers (a sensible
   mix of odd and even, low and high numbers) for the next draw.
4. **It checks itself honestly.** WinLot replays its method on hundreds of past draws and shows how its
   picks would have done compared with numbers chosen completely at random.

## An honest word about predictions

Lottery draws are random. No website, person or computer can know the next winning numbers. Past
results do not change the chances of future draws, and WinLot's own self-check shows its picks do about
as well as random numbers. Treat the suggestions as a fun, informed way to choose numbers you would
otherwise pick by chance. **Please play responsibly and only spend what you can afford.**

WinLot is an independent project. It is not connected to, or approved by, the National Lotteries Board
or the Development Lotteries Board. Always confirm results on the official websites
([nlb.lk](https://www.nlb.lk), [dlb.lk](https://www.dlb.lk)) before claiming a prize.

## Is it free?

Yes. The site is free to use, has no sign-up, and the software itself is open source (see
[License](#license)).
