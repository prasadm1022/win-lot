# WinLot

**Visit the site: [https://winlot.hadesvault.com](https://winlot.hadesvault.com)**

WinLot is a free website that keeps the winning numbers of Sri Lanka's most popular lotteries
(DLB lotteries since April 2021, NLB lotteries since March 2026) and turns them into easy-to-read
charts and suggested numbers for the next draw.

## What lotteries does it cover?

- **National Lotteries Board (NLB):** Dhana Nidhanaya, Mega Power, Govisetha
- **Development Lotteries Board (DLB):** Ada Kotipathi, Super Ball, Shanida, Kapruka

## What can you do on WinLot?

- **See the latest results** for every lottery on one page, with the winning letter and numbers
  shown as lottery balls.
- **See when the jackpot was won.** Draws where we know someone won the jackpot (the Super Prize) are
  marked "Jackpot won", and each lottery has a list of every jackpot win we know about, with the
  current jackpot amount and how long it has been since the last win. DLB publishes no list of
  winners, so its wins are worked out from the advertised jackpot, from now on and two draws late.
- **Get suggested numbers for the next draw.** For each lottery WinLot offers a Top pick and four other
  sets of numbers (and a letter), with a countdown to the next draw.
- **Explore the history.** Browse every past draw, going back to 2021 for DLB lotteries.
- **Understand the patterns** through colourful charts: which numbers came up most and least often,
  which have gone longest without appearing, common number pairs, how often each letter wins, and
  more. These describe past draws only; a draw has no memory, so none of it changes the next draw.
- **Use it anywhere.** It works on phones, tablets and computers, in a dark or light look.

## How does it work?

1. **It collects the results.** Every evening, after the draws, WinLot reads the official results
   published on the NLB and DLB websites and adds them to its own archive. Nobody has to type them in.
   It also records which draws had a jackpot winner.
2. **It weighs three kinds of evidence, most important first.**
   - **Jackpot-winning numbers** count the most: each jackpot-winning result counts like 12 ordinary draws.
     WinLot does not treat them as lucky numbers. It looks at what they have in common, such as which
     tens the numbers fall in, how many are odd, how spread out they are, and the letter.
   - **Jackpot-winning dates** come next: each one counts like 4 draws. WinLot checks whether jackpots
     tend to fall on similar days (for example near the full moon or around payday) and gives draws on
     similar days a little more say.
   - **Ordinary daily results** count the least: once each. They are turned down when needed, so they
     never outweigh the jackpot evidence.
3. **It suggests numbers.** The **Top pick** combines all the evidence known for that lottery (where no
   jackpot win is known yet, that is the daily results alone). Three more sets show what each kind of
   evidence points to on its own; while a kind of evidence is missing or shows no pattern yet (for
   example no jackpot numbers known), its set is a balanced pick from all the evidence instead, clearly
   different from the sets before it, and its note says why. A **Lucky dip** is a random set that
   follows the same rules, for comparison. The sets look like ordinary draws, never repeat a past
   result, and share at most one number with any single past jackpot while only a few jackpots are
   known (the limit loosens a little as more become known).
4. **It checks itself honestly.** WinLot replays its method on hundreds of past draws, each time using
   only what was known before that draw. It shows how its picks would have done compared with numbers
   chosen completely at random, and with its previous method.

## An honest word about predictions

Lottery draws are random. No website, person or computer can know the next winning numbers. Past
results do not change the chances of future draws, and WinLot's own self-check shows its picks do about
as well as random numbers. Jackpot wins depend on which tickets people bought, not on how the balls
fall, so jackpot numbers and dates tell us about players, not about the draw machine. Treat the
suggestions as a fun, informed way to choose numbers you would otherwise pick by chance. **Please play
responsibly and only spend what you can afford.**

WinLot is an independent project. It is not connected to, or approved by, the National Lotteries Board
or the Development Lotteries Board. Always confirm results on the official websites
([nlb.lk](https://www.nlb.lk), [dlb.lk](https://www.dlb.lk)) before claiming a prize.
